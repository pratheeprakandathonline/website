AI of things

AI, applied to real work

A platform I co-built, and agentic AI shaped around the workflows of businesses of different sizes.

Co-founder

Zaper

An AI-native platform for companies that run large field workforces, mainly construction, MEP and facility management across the GCC. It shows where crews are and what they're working on, using GPS, beacons or wearables depending on the site. It also turns workers' voice notes into tasks and updates, and gives managers one clear picture of the day without chasing calls and spreadsheets.

Workflow Orchestration

Agentic AI, one workflow at a time

Most businesses don't need more AI tools. They need someone to work out where AI actually fits, and that's the part I do.

I sit with the team and map how a workflow really runs: who does what, where time goes, and where mistakes creep in. Then I pick the steps an AI agent can take on, and define exactly what each agent should do, what it needs to know, and where a person stays in charge. That becomes a clear blueprint for the AI engineers who build and deploy the agents. I stay with it until it works on the ground.

Some of the workflows I've mapped

  • Customs paperwork for Hellmann An agent that prepares send-ready customs documentation for shipments.
  • Quotation engine for Huda Lighting An agent that reads a customer's RFQ, matches it to Huda's stock, and produces the compliance sheet, estimate and BOQ.
  • WhatsApp order desk for retail shops Voice-note orders in, confirmed orders out, with a simple screen for the shop to confirm and assign.
